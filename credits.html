Standing on decades of C64 preservation work.

POKE64 combines a native iPad frontend with established open-source emulation technology.

VICE

The Commodore 64 emulation core used by POKE64 is based on VICE / vice-libretro. VICE is a long-running open-source emulator project covering Commodore computer systems and historical peripherals.

Exact revision information, source availability and third-party license notices are maintained in the POKE64 source repository.
